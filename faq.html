FAQ

Your questions, answered clearly.

Is Project Bob available in the browser?

Yes. It is designed for the browser and works with local image processing tools.

Do my images leave the device?

No. The current workflow is designed to keep the project local in-browser for privacy and speed.

Can I export my work?

Yes. You can save a PNG export or download an outline version.

Does it work on touch screens?

Yes, the workspace supports pointer input for responsive drawing and editing.
